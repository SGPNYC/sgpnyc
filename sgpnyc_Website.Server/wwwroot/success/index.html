ICBC

DOX

The Challenge

ICBC needed a system they could rely on for document and covenant compliance. All banking businesses are audited and expected to keep a firm record of their data and transactions. Many financial companies solely rely on Excel, when they also need a system that provides substantial security and is adequate for daily team navigation. Document and covenant compliance is crucial for ICBC because it tracks and compiles their data and requirements for each client.

The Solution

For ICBC, SGP's DOX is customized specifically to the international bank's needs. DOX is tailored to the partnering organization's requirements. Working with ICBC, DOX creates reports, arranges data, highlights trends, and publishes results. DOX tracks all deadlines and pass/fail covenant tests online with a dashboard that supplies each user a snapshot of past-due and upcoming requirements. Different information is available for each user depending on their clearance within ICBC.

Aareal Bank

DASH

The Challenge

Aareal Bank spent so much time weekly creating and generating reports for their loans and transactions. There was also significant time spent each day collecting and analyzing data, and they needed a secure system to share information and audit their requirements.

The Solution

SGP's DASH system allows Aareal to implement information acquired from their data more quickly into daily operations. It saves Aareal the time spent creating reports by providing a record system that generates analytics through adjustable reporting tools. DASH's interface allows Aareal to rely on an in-house system, keeping their requirements and company input secure.

Lincoln Center Theatre

The Vivian Beaumont Theater & The Mitzi E. Newhouse Theater

Custom

The Challenge

The challenge Lincoln Center Theater faced began with tracking house seat arrangements and their sales for each show. The theater company spent an excessive amount on house seating scheduling and organization. They also lacked the ability to consistently collect data from ticket sales, especially to hone in on specific customer purchases and production timelines.

The Solution

For Lincoln Center Theater, the SGP system organizes and analyzes house seat schedules and sales. A customer's ability to purchase a house seat ticket is directly activated through SGP's software. The system tracks how often customers purchase tickets, the time of occurrence, and the sales amount. SGP software is regularly used for monthly productions as well as The Tony's — tracking the TONY Voter list since their hit musical Contact in the late 90's.

Clark Transfer

Custom

The Challenge

"Let's Get the Show on the Road" — Clark Transfer's company had expanded over 70 years and needed a system that would support their vast workflow. There was a tremendous need for in-house support and accessible technology in daily operations. The company had hundreds of trailers, routes, drivers, productions, clients, and a timeline to organize and manage daily.

The Solution

SGP designed a custom system to accomplish three objectives: (1) Track trailer locations via a customized GPS system with updates every few minutes and a visual MOVE Board showing active and inactive trailers. (2) Track sales and financial aspects of production transportation. (3) Regulate detailed driver invoice records — on the back end the company controls payment vouchers, and on the front end drivers sign into a portal to fill in schedule information.
